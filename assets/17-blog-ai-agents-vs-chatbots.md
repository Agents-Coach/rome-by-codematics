## What Is a Chatbot?

A chatbot is a computer program that follows rules. It does what you program it to do.

**How a chatbot works:**
1. You build a flow (in a visual builder or code)
2. You define conditions: "If someone says X, respond with Y"
3. The chatbot checks each incoming message against your conditions
4. If there's a match, it sends the programmed response
5. If there's no match, it either sends a default message or fails

**Examples of chatbot platforms:**
- ManyChat
- Landbot
- Wati (basic automation)
- Typebot
- Landbot

**What chatbots are good at:**
- Answering FAQs with fixed responses
- Routing people to the right team
- Sending promotional messages
- Simple welcome/away messages

**What chatbots can't do:**
- Understand a message that's slightly different from your programmed flow
- Qualify leads without manual tagging
- Handle objections that weren't in your flow
- Have genuine conversations
- Learn from context

---

## What Is an AI Sales Agent?

An AI sales agent is a system that uses artificial intelligence (not rules) to handle conversations.

**How an AI sales agent works:**
1. You tell the AI about your business (what you sell, your price, your FAQs)
2. The AI understands natural language — it doesn't need exact keyword matches
3. When someone messages, the AI reads the full message, understands intent, and responds appropriately
4. The AI can qualify leads, handle objections, and book calls without pre-programmed flows
5. The AI uses context from the full conversation to respond naturally
