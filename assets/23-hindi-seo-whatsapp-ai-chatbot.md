## WhatsApp AI Chatbot Kya Hota Hai?

Basic chatbot ek rule-based system hota hai. Matlab aap usse batate ho ki agar koi "price" likhe toh kya reply karo.

WhatsApp AI Chatbot alag hai. Yeh AI (Artificial Intelligence) use karta hai — jo samajh sakta hai ki message kya bol raha hai, context kya hai, aur sahi reply kya dena hai.

**Ek example se samjho:**

Basic chatbot: "Price" likho → "Our price is ₹45,000" bhejo. Sirf itna.

AI Chatbot: "Bhai, kitna lagta hai yaar? Thoda expensive lag raha hai..." → Samjho → "Ji, program ₹45,000 hai but 3 EMI mein bhi ho sakta hai — ₹15,000/month. Kya aapko details bhejun? 👇"

AI chatbot samajhta hai ki "expensive" ka matlab kya hai, context samajhta hai, aur relevant reply deta hai.
