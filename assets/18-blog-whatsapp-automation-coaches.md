WhatsApp Business is a free app from Meta, designed specifically for small and medium businesses.

## Part 3: The Three Levels of WhatsApp Automation

### Level 1: Built-in Automation (Free)

WhatsApp Business has free built-in automation tools:

**Greeting Messages:**
When someone messages you for the first time or after 14 days, WhatsApp Business can send an automatic greeting:
> "Hey! Thanks for reaching out. I personally handle my messages and will get back to you shortly."

**Away Messages:**
When you're offline (outside business hours), WhatsApp Business sends an automatic reply:
> "Hi! I'm currently unavailable. I'll respond within 24 hours."

**Quick Replies:**
Save common responses and access them with a slash command:
> Type "/pricing" → your pricing message appears

**Best for:** Coaches who just need basic "I'm unavailable" messages.

### Level 2: Chatbot Automation (₹500–₹10,000/month)

Tools like Wati, ManyChat, and similar platforms let you build chatbots on WhatsApp.

**What chatbots do:**
- Respond to keywords with pre-written messages
- Route people to different flows
- Send promotional broadcasts
- Handle basic FAQ automation

**What chatbots can't do:**
- Understand questions outside your programmed flows
- Qualify leads without manual tagging
- Handle objections in real-time
- Book calls automatically

**Setup time:** 1–8 hours of flow building.

**Best for:** Coaches who want broadcast campaigns and basic FAQ automation.

**What AI sales agents do:**

## Part 4: How to Choose the Right Automation Level

Not every coach needs the same level of automation. Here's how to decide:

### Choose Built-in Automation If:
- You get fewer than 5 inquiry messages per day
- You're comfortable manually qualifying and following up

### Choose Chatbot Automation If:

## Part 6: What Coaches Should Automate (And What to Keep Human)

### Automate These:
- Initial inquiry responses
- FAQ answers
- Pricing information
- Objection responses ("it's too expensive", "I need to think about it")
- Scheduling and booking
- Lead qualification questions

### Keep Human:
- Deep discovery conversations
- Coaching sessions themselves
- Sensitive or emotional conversations
- Complex negotiations
- Contract and payment discussions

**The rule:** Automate the conversations that cost you time but don't require your unique expertise. Keep human the conversations where your coaching expertise is irreplaceable.

## Part 7: Common Mistakes Coaches Make with WhatsApp Automation

### Mistake 1: Automating Before You're Clear on Your Offer
Rome can only automate conversations about your coaching if you know what you're selling. Be clear on:
- What your program is
- Who it's for
- What it costs
- What the next step is

### Mistake 2: Using Personal WhatsApp
Using personal WhatsApp for automation violates WhatsApp's Terms of Service. Your account can be banned. Always use WhatsApp Business.

### Mistake 3: Setting and Forgetting
AI automation works best when you review it and improve it. Check your conversation history weekly. Add new FAQs as questions come up. Rome learns from configuration — update it regularly.

### Mistake 4: Trying to Automate Everything
Some conversations need a human. If someone is having a crisis or needs deep guidance, Rome flags the conversation for you to take over. That's not a failure — it's designed that way.

### Mistake 5: Ignoring Follow-Up
Automation handles the first message. But leads who don't convert in the first exchange still need follow-up. Set a system for following up with leads Rome qualified but didn't close.
