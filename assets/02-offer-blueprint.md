# Offer Blueprint — Rome by Codematics
**Offer Lead Output** | 2026-06-04

---

## Core Offer Redefined

**What Rome actually sells:**
Not "WhatsApp automation" — that sounds technical.
Rome sells: **"Never miss another hot lead while you sleep."**

**Primary offer frame:** AI Sales Agent subscription
**Secondary offer frame:** 24/7 WhatsApp receptionist that qualifies leads and books calls

---

## Revised Offer Stack

### Tier 1: Starter — $47/mo
**Name:** "Rome Starter"
**For:** Solo coaches, freelancers, consultants with one WhatsApp number

**What's included:**
- 1 WhatsApp Business number connected
- 500 AI responses/month
- Full AI agent configuration (name, offer, FAQs, objections)
- Chat history (90 days)
- Email support

**What's NOT included:**
- Calendar booking
- Human handoff
- Priority support
- Additional WhatsApp numbers

**Ideal customer:** Has 1 WhatsApp Business number, gets 5–20 messages/day, misses leads

**Anchor message:** "For the coach who needs one WhatsApp number handled."

---

### Tier 2: Pro — $97/mo
**Name:** "Rome Pro"
**For:** Growing coaches, consultants with multiple offers or audiences

**Everything in Starter, plus:**
- 3 WhatsApp Business numbers
- 2,000 AI responses/month
- Calendly/calendar integration (Rome sends booking links automatically)
- Lead analytics dashboard
- Conversation tagging + export
- Priority email support (24h response)

**Anchor message:** "For the coach who needs more reach and wants leads booked automatically."

---

### Tier 3: Agency — $197/mo
**Name:** "Rome Agency"
**For:** Coaches running multiple clients, or agency owners reselling Rome

**Everything in Pro, plus:**
- 10 WhatsApp Business numbers
- 10,000 AI responses/month
- Chatwoot human handoff (real agent takes over live)
- Custom agent training (we configure Rome for your client's niche)
- Dedicated Slack channel support
- White-label ready (branded dashboard)

**Anchor message:** "For the coach with clients — or the agency running Rome for others."

---

## Fast-Cash Entry Offer

**Name:** "Rome 7-Day Sprint"
**Price:** $1 (or free with credit card capture)
**Duration:** 7 days full Pro access, no credit card required upfront

**Purpose:** Remove all friction from trial. Connect WhatsApp → Rome runs → see leads captured → upgrade.

**Conversion goal:** 20% of sprint users convert to Starter or Pro

---

## Upsell Logic

### Trial → Starter
- After 7 days, Rome sends a "your week in review" email showing leads captured, messages handled, calls booked
- CTA: "Keep Rome running — Starter at $47/mo"

### Starter → Pro
- When Starter hits 80% of message limit (400 messages), send upgrade prompt
- Show exactly how many leads were missed due to limit
- CTA: "Unlock 3 more numbers + calendar booking"

### Pro → Agency
- When user tries to add a 4th WhatsApp number, upsell triggers
- Show "You're outgrowing Starter. Here's what Agency gives you."

"If Rome doesn't help you capture at least 5 qualified leads in 30 days, email us — we'll refund your first month. No questions."

This removes the "what if it doesn't work" objection entirely.

## Pricing Logic

**Anchor:** $47/mo (Starter) — makes $97 and $197 feel like natural upgrades

**Price vs. alternatives:**
- VA: $200–$2,000/mo → Rome at $47 is 4–40x cheaper
- Wati: ₹999–₹9,999/mo (~$12–$120/mo) → Rome Pro at $97 is competitive for value
- ManyChat: $15–$155/mo → Rome Pro at $97 offers more AI value

**Value anchor message:**
"You spend more on coffee in a month than Rome costs. And Rome brings in leads while you're sleeping."
