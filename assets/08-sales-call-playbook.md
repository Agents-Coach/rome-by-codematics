# Sales Call Playbook — Rome by Codematics
**Sales Lead Output** | 2026-06-04

---

## SALES MODEL
**Type:** Self-serve SaaS (primary) + Human demo call (secondary)
**Primary sales:** Direct purchase on website — no human required
**Secondary sales:** Demo call for hesitant buyers, Agency tier, enterprise inquiries

---

## CALL TYPE 1: DISCOVERY CALL (20 minutes)

**Purpose:** Convert hesitant visitors into trial starters
**Who takes:** Founder or sales rep at Codematics
**When to offer:** After trial signup with no WhatsApp connect, after demo request from /demo page

---

### Pre-Call Prep
- Pull up lead's profile: name, what they do, why they signed up
- Check if they connected WhatsApp (if not, ask why)
- Review any messages in chat history
- Prepare: 3 discovery questions + Rome demo

---

### Call Script

**Opening (0:00–1:00)**
"Thanks for hopping on a call, [Name]. This won't take long — 20 minutes max.

Before I show you anything, I want to understand your situation.

Are you okay if I ask you a few quick questions?"

**[Wait for yes]**

---

**Discovery Questions (1:00–8:00)**

**Q1: "Tell me about your practice. What do you do and who do you serve?"**

*[Listen. Don't sell. Build rapport.]*

**Q2: "How are you currently handling WhatsApp messages from potential clients?"**

*[Listen for pain: manually responding, losing leads, no time, using a VA, using Wati/other tool]*

**Q3: "What happens when someone messages you at 10 PM and you see it at 8 AM the next day?"**

*[This is the pain hook. Let them feel it.]*

**Follow-up: "How many of those 2 AM leads have you lost to competitors?"**

*[Silence. Let them answer. This is the pain amplifier.]*

---

**Demo (8:00–15:00)**

"Now let me show you what Rome does. This is the WhatsApp number for one of our coaches — Priya. Watch what happens when someone messages her.

*[Pull up WhatsApp or show demo.]*

See? Three seconds. Rome replied before I could even set my coffee down.

And look at what Rome did — quoted the price, offered to send details. Didn't need Priya for that.

Now watch what happens when someone says they're interested.

*[Show Calendly booking.]*

Rome sent the Calendly link automatically. The lead booked. Priya woke up to a confirmed call.

That's Rome."

---

**Objection Handling (15:00–18:00)**

**Objection: "I don't think my audience is ready for AI."**
> "That's fair. But here's what's happening — your audience is already talking to AI. On every website, every business account. They're used to it. What they're NOT used to is getting a reply at 2 AM from a real person. Rome gives them that."

**Objection: "I'm worried about WhatsApp banning my number."**
> "That's the right concern. We only work with WhatsApp Business accounts — not personal WhatsApp. We follow Meta's policies. Thousands of businesses use WhatsApp automation today."

**Objection: "I tried something like this before and it was too complicated."**
> "That's exactly why we built Rome to be different. There's no flow builder. No drag-and-drop. You tell us what you sell, we set it up. 10 minutes, you're live."

**Objection: "Let me think about it."**
> "Completely understand. What specifically do you want to think through? Is it the price, the setup, or something else?"

---

**Close (18:00–20:00)**

"So here's what I'd suggest: let's just get you set up. It's free for 7 days. No credit card.

You scan a QR code, configure Rome in 10 minutes, and let it run for a week.

See what Rome captures. If it doesn't work for you, we part ways. No hard feelings.

If it does work — which it does for most coaches — you decide if the ₹4,000/month is worth it.

Sound fair?"

*[Get commitment: "I'll send you a link right now to get started."]*

---

### Post-Call Actions
- Send Slack/WhatsApp DM to lead with trial link
- Send follow-up email within 1 hour: "Here's what we discussed + trial link"
- If not converted: follow up in 3 days with ROI stat ("Rome users capture 23 leads on average in week 1")
- If not converted after 2nd follow-up: send case study + soft close

## SALES OBJECTION LIBRARY

### Top 5 Objections (and responses)

**1. "I need to talk to my partner/partner first."**
> "Totally. Can I send you something to share with them so they can see exactly what Rome does? A quick video or our comparison page?"

**2. "My clients aren't on WhatsApp."**
> "Interesting — where do your clients find you? [Listen] And when they decide to reach out, where do they message you? [Listen] That's where Rome works. If WhatsApp isn't your channel, Rome isn't the right fit. But if it is — Rome is the fastest way to handle it."

**3. "It's too expensive for where I am right now."**
> "I hear you. Can I ask — how much is a single new client worth to you? [Listen] If Rome helps you capture just one extra client a month, that's ROI in the first week. What would one new client a month change for you?"

**4. "I'm not technical enough for this."**
> "That's actually Rome's biggest selling point. You don't need to be technical. You don't need to build anything. You just tell us what you sell. We do the rest. My team sets up the AI, you just scan a QR code. That's it."

**5. "Let me see some reviews first."**
> "Absolutely. Let me send you a few testimonials from coaches in your space. [Send case studies] And honestly — the best review is trying it yourself. 7 days, free, no commitment."
