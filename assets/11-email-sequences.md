## EMAIL SEQUENCE RULES

- **Send between 9 AM – 11 AM IST** (highest open rates for Indian audience)
- **A/B test subject lines** — emoji vs no emoji, question vs statement
- **Plain text style** — feels like a human wrote it, not a marketing team
- **One CTA per email** — no competing CTAs
- **Personalize with [Name]** — first name at minimum
- **Plain text emails convert better** — avoid HTML-heavy designs
