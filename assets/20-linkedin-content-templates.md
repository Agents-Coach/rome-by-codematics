## LINKEDIN BEST PRACTICES

### Timing
- Best days: Tuesday–Thursday
- Best time: 7–9 AM IST (catches people on morning commute)
- Second best: 12–1 PM IST (lunch browsing)

### Engagement
- Respond to every comment within 2 hours of posting
- Ask a question at the end of every post (drives comments)
- Share posts to relevant LinkedIn newsletters
- Comment meaningfully on 5 other posts before posting your own

### Metrics to Track
- Impressions (reach)
- Engagement rate (likes + comments / impressions)
- Click-through rate (link clicks / impressions)
- Leads generated (track with UTM parameters)

### Hashtag Strategy
Use 3–5 relevant hashtags per post:
- #WhatsAppBusiness
- #AIAssistant
- #CoachingBusiness
- #DigitalTransformation
- #SalesAutomation
- #IndianEntrepreneurs
- #Solopreneur
