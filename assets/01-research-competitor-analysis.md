### Direct Competitor Deep-Dive

#### Wati.io (India-focused, biggest threat)
- **Pricing:** ₹999–₹9,999/mo (~$12–$120 USD)
- **Strengths:** WhatsApp-native, team inbox, CRM, cheap for India market, Zapier integration
- **Weaknesses:** No AI agent, rule-based automation only, template-heavy, requires manual flow building
- **Target:** Indian SMEs, coaches, small agencies
- **Rome's angle vs Wati:** Wati requires flow building. Rome requires ONE prompt. Wati doesn't have Claude AI. Rome IS the AI.

#### ManyChat
- **Pricing:** $15–$155/mo
- **Strengths:** Multi-channel (Instagram, FB, WA), growth tools, analytics
- **Weaknesses:** WhatsApp is one channel among many, not purpose-built, AI features require expensive plans
- **Target:** Agencies, creators, ecommerce
- **Rome's angle vs ManyChat:** ManyChat's AI is an add-on. Rome IS the AI. ManyChat has no sales agent focus.

#### Typebot
- **Pricing:** Free (self-hosted) / €89/mo (cloud)
- **Strengths:** Open-source, beautiful, highly customizable
- **Weaknesses:** Requires technical setup, self-hosting headache, not WhatsApp-first, no AI agent out of the box
- **Target:** Developers, technical marketers
- **Rome's angle vs Typebot:** Typebot needs flow building. Rome is zero-flow-build. Rome is ready-to-use, not ready-to-configure.

### Pricing Benchmarks
- Entry-level WhatsApp SaaS: $15–$49/mo
- Mid-tier: $50–$150/mo
- Enterprise: $150–$500+/mo
- Rome's pricing ($47/$97/$197): Competitive at entry, strong value at Pro vs. competitors

### Underserved Market Segments
1. **Indian solo coaches** — Wati is too manual, no AI
2. **Non-technical service providers** — need AI, not flow builders
3. **Sleeping businesses** — need 24/7 AI response, not just automation
4. **Low-to-mid budget buyers** — $47/mo is accessible for coaches earning $500–$2000/client

### Competitive Moat for Rome
1. **Claude-native AI** — not rule-based, not GPT-3.5 fallback
2. **One-prompt setup** — competitors require flow building
3. **Sales-agent focus** — not support, not marketing, not broadcasting
4. **Codematics infrastructure** — self-hosted, compliance-friendly, own data
5. **Indian market timing** — WhatsApp is the #1 biz tool, AI adoption is accelerating

## PART 2: BUYER PSYCHOLOGY DEEP-DIVE

### Primary Buyer Persona

**Name:** "The Overwhelmed Coach"

**Demographics:**
- Age: 28–50
- Location: India (Tier 1–3 cities), some global
- Income: ₹5L–₹50L/year from coaching/consulting/freelancing
- Platform: WhatsApp Business as primary client communication tool
- Tech level: Can use smartphones, Canva, Google Forms — NOT developers

**Psychographics:**
- Entrepreneurial, growth-oriented
- Values time over money
- Frustrated with admin work stealing from client work
- Skeptical of AI but curious
- Has tried chatbots before and found them "too complicated"

### Exact Pain Points (in buyer language)

**Pain 1: Message overwhelm**
> "I get 20–30 WhatsApp messages a day. Half are the same questions. I spend 2 hours every evening just answering WhatsApp."

**Pain 2: Missing hot leads**
> "I was in a meeting and someone messaged me at 10 PM asking about my course. I replied at 9 AM the next day. They already bought from someone else."

**Pain 3: Repeating the same answers**
> "I literally copy-paste the same reply about 20 times a day. 'Hi, yes the course is open, here is the link, payment is this, any questions?' It kills me."

**Pain 4: Can't afford help**
> "I can't afford a VA right now. I earn ₹3 lakhs a month and a good VA costs ₹15,000–₹20,000/month. It's not worth it for just WhatsApp."

**Pain 5: Tried chatbots, gave up**
> "I tried ManyChat last year. Spent 3 hours building a flow. It still sounded robotic. Deleted it."

### What Makes Them Say YES
1. **Seeing proof someone like them uses it** — peer social proof from coaches
2. **"5 minute setup" claim** — simplicity is the #1 differentiator they care about
3. **Price feels like a "no-brainer"** — $47/mo vs. a VA at $200/mo
4. **Seeing a WhatsApp reply from Rome** — if they can test it on their own phone
5. **Human fallback option** — knowing a real person can take over

### What Makes Them Say NO
1. **"It's too complicated"** — fear of setup complexity
2. **"Will WhatsApp ban me?"** — safety concern about their business number
3. **"AI sounds fake"** — authenticity concern about the brand
4. **"I don't trust online payments"** — if Stripe/online checkout feels risky
5. **"Let me think about it"** — no urgency, no compelling reason to act now

### Decision Triggers
- A competitor raised prices → time to switch
- A coach they follow promoted WhatsApp automation → aspirational trigger
- Missed a big client because of late reply → pain trigger
- Read a Reddit thread about AI assistants → awareness trigger
- WhatsApp announced new business features → ecosystem validation

### Discovery Channels
1. **WhatsApp** — they live here, no escaping it
2. **YouTube** — "how to automate WhatsApp business" searches
3. **LinkedIn** — coaches and consultants, especially Indian
4. **Instagram** — younger coaches, creators
5. **Reddit** — r/Entrepreneur, r/India, r/coaching
6. **Google** — "automate WhatsApp business messages"
7. **Word of mouth** — WhatsApp group referrals

### Key Insights for Copy
- Lead with **time savings**, not features
- Use their exact words: "I miss leads at night", "same questions", "copy-paste"
- Show the WhatsApp chat as the proof — visual proof beats text
- Address the WhatsApp ban fear immediately in copy
- Price anchor against VA cost, not against "monthly software"
