## CASE STUDY TEMPLATE

### Case Study: Priya — Career Coach, Mumbai

**Headline:** From Missing Leads at 2 AM to Waking Up to Booked Calls

**The Situation:**
Priya runs a career coaching practice in Mumbai. She works with senior professionals transitioning into leadership roles. Her coaching program is priced at ₹45,000 for 12 weeks.

**The Problem:**
Priya was getting 15–20 WhatsApp messages a day from potential clients. Most asked the same questions: "Is the program open?", "What does it cost?", "When does it start?"

She was spending 2 hours every evening replying to WhatsApp — time she should have been preparing for sessions or acquiring new clients.

Worse: she missed 3–4 hot leads a month because she replied too late.

**The Solution:**
Priya signed up for Rome. She connected her WhatsApp Business number (5 minutes, one QR scan). She configured Rome with her offer details, FAQs, and pricing (10 minutes).

Rome went live immediately.

**The Results (30 days):**
- 147 messages handled by Rome
- 23 qualified leads identified
- 8 booked discovery calls
- 4 new clients enrolled
- ₹1,80,000 in new revenue
- Priya's WhatsApp response time: 3 seconds (Rome)
- Priya's time spent on WhatsApp: from 2 hours/day to 20 minutes/day

**ROI:**
- Rome cost: ₹4,000/month
- New revenue: ₹1,80,000
- ROI: 45x

**Quote:**
> "I used to dread checking WhatsApp. Now I wake up and Rome has already done the work. I just show up to booked calls."
> — Priya Sharma, Career Coach, Mumbai
