## WHERE ROME WINS

### Rome wins on: AI Native Sales

Wati is a team inbox tool that added a chatbot. Rome is an AI sales agent that happens to use WhatsApp.

The difference:
- Wati: You build a flow → bot follows the flow → if message doesn't match flow, bot fails
- Rome: You tell Rome what you sell → Rome understands and responds → handles anything

**For coaches who don't want to build flows, Rome is dramatically simpler.**

### Rome wins on: Lead Qualification

Wati requires you to manually tag leads and qualify them. Rome does it automatically using Claude's reasoning.

Example:
- Wati: Lead says "I'm interested in your coaching program" → you manually tag as HOT
- Rome: Lead says "I'm interested" → Rome identifies as HOT → sends Calendly → lead books call → you get notification

### Rome wins on: Objection Handling

Wati can't handle objections without pre-built flows. Rome has a configurable objection map — you tell Rome how to respond to "it's too expensive" and it handles it naturally.

### Rome wins on: Pricing Transparency

Wati's pricing has hidden complexity: extra charges for extra numbers, broadcasts, API access. Rome's pricing is simple: per-plan, all-inclusive.

---

## WHERE WATI WINS

### Wati wins on: Team Collaboration
If you have a team handling WhatsApp (multiple agents), Wati's team inbox is superior. Rome is designed for solo operators or single-agent businesses.

**When to recommend Wati over Rome:**
- You have a team of 5+ people handling WhatsApp
- You need broadcast campaigns (Wati excels here)
- You want drag-and-drop chatbot building (you like building flows)
- You don't need AI (basic automation is fine)

### Wati wins on: Market Trust
Wati has been around since 2021. They have 10,000+ users, press coverage, and brand recognition. Rome is new.

**Mitigation:** Rome's positioning should emphasize "built on Claude AI" — the technology itself provides credibility.
