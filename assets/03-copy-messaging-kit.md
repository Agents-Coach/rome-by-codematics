## OBJECTION COPY (5 major objections)

### Objection 1: "Will WhatsApp ban my number?"
**Response:** Rome uses the official WhatsApp Business API — the same technology used by thousands of small businesses. We follow Meta's policies. We recommend using WhatsApp Business (not personal WhatsApp) for maximum compliance. Thousands of coaches run automation on WhatsApp today.

### Objection 2: "It's too complicated to set up."
**Response:** Scan a QR code. That's it. Rome connects to your existing WhatsApp Business number. Then answer 10 questions about what you sell. Rome is live. Total time: 10 minutes.

### Objection 3: "AI sounds fake and robotic."
**Response:** Rome is powered by Claude — one of the most advanced AI models available. But more importantly, you configure Rome to sound exactly like you. Your name, your tone, your offer description. Rome sounds like you because you trained it to.

### Objection 4: "I don't want AI talking to my leads."
**Response:** Rome doesn't replace you — it handles the conversations you're losing anyway. The 2 AM messages. The repeat questions. The basic qualifying. When Rome captures a lead or identifies a hot prospect, you take over. And at any time, a lead can type "talk to human" and a real person responds.

### Objection 5: "It's too expensive."
**Response:** Rome costs $47/mo. A part-time VA to answer WhatsApp costs $300–$2,000/mo. Rome handles the messages while you charge clients ₹5,000–₹50,000 for your services. One new client pays for years of Rome.
