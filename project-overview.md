## 4. Audience

**Primary Audience:** Indian coaches, consultants, course creators, agency owners (25–50)
**Secondary Audience:** Global solopreneurs, freelancers with WhatsApp-based businesses

**Buyer / User Stage:** Problem-aware (they know they need automation) + early solution-aware

**Top Problems:**
- Wastes hours repeating answers to the same WhatsApp messages
- Misses hot leads at night / during meetings
- Can't afford a VA or sales team
- Existing chatbots are too complex to set up

**Top Desired Outcomes:**
- Hot leads booked on calls automatically
- 24/7 WhatsApp response
- Simple setup (not a 40-hour chatbot build)

**Top Objections / Friction:**
- "Will WhatsApp ban my number?"
- "Is this legal/compliant?"
- "It's too complicated to set up"
- "My audience isn't on WhatsApp"
- "I don't trust AI to talk to my leads"
