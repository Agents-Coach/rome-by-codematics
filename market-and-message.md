# Market & Message — Rome by Codematics

## Primary Market
Indian coaches, consultants, course creators, agency owners (25–50) who use WhatsApp Business for client communication.

## Positioning
**Position:** "The AI sales rep that works while you sleep"

**Category:** WhatsApp automation + AI sales agent

**Direct competitors:**
- Typebot / Landbot (complex flow builders, not AI-native)
- Wati / Intercom (expensive, enterprise, no AI sales agent)
- Voiceflow (complex, not WhatsApp-first)
- ManyChat (social media focus, not WhatsApp-native sales)

**Differentiation:**
- 1-prompt setup (not a chatbot builder)
- AI-native (Claude-powered, not rule-based)
- Sales-focused (not support-focused)
- Ultra-simple for non-technical users
- 3-second response time

## Core Message Pillars
1. **Speed:** Replies in <3 seconds, 24/7
2. **Simplicity:** 5-minute setup, no tech skills needed
3. **Results:** Qualified leads, booked calls, sales while sleeping
4. **Price:** $47/mo vs $500+/mo for a VA

## Brand Voice
- Bold, direct, practical
- Warm but confident (not overly playful)
- Growth-oriented, operator mindset
- No hype, no buzzwords
- Conversational on WhatsApp

## Rome Character
- Name: Rome
- Personality: Helpful, efficient, never pushy
- Tone: Warm professional (configurable: friendly / professional / casual)
- Avatar: Emerald green, premium, clean
- Tagline: "Your AI Sales Rep. Works 24/7. Runs on WhatsApp."

## Buyer Language
- "I get messages all day asking the same thing"
- "I miss leads at night"
- "I need someone to handle WhatsApp for me"
- "Too busy to reply to everyone"
- "AI chatbots are too complicated"
- "I just want it to work"
